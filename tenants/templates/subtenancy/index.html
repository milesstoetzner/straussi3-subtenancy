I want to rent a room for a short period:

If you want to sublet your room, please check first the following points:

  • You must rent a room through the dorm referent(WoRe):
    We are officially authorized from the Studierendenwerk Stuttgart (SWS) to manage all short-term renting in the dormitory and we work as agent to make it easy for student to quickly find a tenant for their rooms. every other way of renting a room is considered(schwarzmiete).
  • You are only allowed to rent a from the main tenant of that room:
    Only tenant who have a rental contract with the SWS can sub rent their room and only during the official rental Period. Without any exceptions.
  • You are only allowed to sub rent a room for 3 Months
  • Sub rental contract can only be signed during the Sprechtstunde of the WoRe:
    You and the person who will rent you his room must be present when signing the contract and if that is not possible, you should contact us to try to find a solution.
  • We offer deposit contracts that we can (at will) confirm and other services that makes the whole process easy for you.
  • The sub rental contract must be signed in the Sprechstunde at least 1 week before the rent period starts. After that the sub rent is no longer possible.
Am I allowed to sub rent a room?

You are qualified to rent a room in a student dormitory., when at least two of the following criteria are fulfilled:

  • You must be a registered student (during the complete sub rent period) at the university of Stuttgart and perusing a Bachelors, masters or an equivalent degree.
    PhDs and researchers are not allowed to live in the student dormitory
  • You must have a valid visa/residence permit to live in Germany.
  • You must be a registered student at a university somewhere in the world, doing a language course in Stuttgart or doing a mandatory Internship for his studies.

The WoRe needs to check your fulfillment to the criteria with written confirmation. So please bring all the needed document to the Sprechstunde.

Where can I find a room?

There three main ways to do that:

  • You already know someone who is eligible to sub rent his/her room and you decide to rent his/her room.
  • You submit a request through our request form and hope that someone will choose you. Then we will send him your contact Information and he will contact you.
  • You can find someone online in the websites designated to such purpose. Be aware that the demand for a room there is very very high.
  • Please clarify everything with each other before you come to our office. you don’t want any surprises in the Sprechstunde.
The process of sub renting a room

After you went through all the above and you are ready to a your room:

  1. You and the main tenant should come to the Sprechstunde and bring all the necessary documents (see below for a list of documents).
  2. If requested from the main tenant your should pay a deposit for the room and you will get it back at the end of the rental:
    1. If so please bring the money to the Sprechstunde. the sum must be at least equivalent to the deposit payed by the main tenant to the SWS. Alternative you can bring along a money transfer confirmation and the main tenant must confirm that he/she got the money.
    2. The WoRe will help fill out and sign the deposit contract.
    3. The Wore will confirm the deposit as an independent third party.
  3. You must sign a document that confirms that the room furniture is there, complete, and Okay(of course you should check it out before signing). within 14 days, the you can complain and the Hausmeister will interevent to clarify the issue.
  4. The WoRe will help you fill out all necessary document and contract.
  5. You should pay a donation of 2,5€ for every rented week to the WoRe. It is only so that the WoRe finance itself and able to function. Only cash is accepted.
  6. The WoRe will officially confirm the sub rental and will hand out written confirmations. Both tenants will receive copies and the original will be kept with us, for as needed in the future.
  7.  The rent cost muss be transferred from the subtenant to the main tenant every first day of the month.
  8.  The key handover should be organized between the subtenants.
  9.  Ending of the sub rental:
    1.  Option1: return the room to the main tenant after the rental is over .the main tenant has 14 days to report any damaged furniture. The Hausmeister will examine the damage and decide about the return of the deposit.
    2.  Option2: yor return the room directly to the SWS. you has a period of 14 day to arrange a meeting with the Hausmeister to handover the room. the Hausmeister will confirm the room handover and that no damage has occurred. And with that you should get the deposit as soon as possible.
Required documents:

All document must be present when signing the contract. There is no “i’ll bring them later”

  • Documents required from the main tenant:
    • Rental Contract
    • Personal ID or A passport (for foreign students: they have to have a valid Visa)
  • Documents required from the subtenant:
    • A valid Personal ID with a Picture.(bus ticket, or gym membership ID is a no-go)
    • For foreign student who require a Visa to live in Germany please bring your valid Visa with you(either on the Passport or separate document)
    • Official proof of enrollment from a university: the document must state your Name, Major and enrollment period. For example: Students at the university of Stuttgart have an ID with the picture and the Semester-marke that they get every semester. together they present a valid proof of enrollment.Other universities have it different but the same basic Information musst be always stated on the document. For special cases you can ask your University to issue such document for you.

Attention

The sum of all subtenancies MUST NOT exceed 3 months! An extension can be requested from our contact person at the SWS. Each case is handled independently. Therefore you have to argue and prove why an extension is required.

I want to sublet my room for a limited period:

if you want to rent your room for a short period, the WoRe(Wohnheimsreferendariat) will help you throughout the whole process. But before you start you should know these important rules if you want to sublet your room, please check first the following points:

  1. you must rent your room through the dorm referent(WoRe):We are officially authorized from the Studierendenwerk Stuttgart (SWS) to manage all short-term renting in the dormitory and we work as agent to make it easy for student to quickly find a tenant for their rooms. every other way of renting your room is considered(schwarzmiete) and may in some cases cause to lose your room. further details about the consequences are in your rent contract with the SWS
  2. you are only allowed to rent your room if are the main tenant of that room: Only tenant who have a rental contract with the SWS can sub rent their room and only during the official rental Period. Without any exceptions.
  3. you are only allowed to sub rent your room for 3 Months:You can sub rent your room for 3 months at a time or more than once for a shorter period but the total must not exceed 3 months. If you have a good reason you must rent your room for a longer period, then you must contact the SWS and ask for a special permission.  You must bring the written permission with you when you sign your sub rental contract.
  4. Sub rental contract can only be signed during the Sprechstunde of the WoRe: You and the person who will rent your room must be present when signing the contract and if that is not possible, you should contact us to try to find a solution.
  5. we offer deposit contracts that we can (at will) confirm and other services that makes the whole process easy for you.
  6. the sub rental contract must be signed in the Sprechstunde at least 1 week before the rent period starts. After that the sub rent is no longer possible.
To whom am I allowed to sub rent my room:

You can rent your room to anyone who is qualified to rent a room in a student dormitory. He/she must fulfill at least two of the following criteria:

  2. He/she must have valid visa/residence permit to live in Germany.
  3. He/she must be a registered student at a university somewhere in the world and he/she is doing a language course in Stuttgart or doing a mandatory Internship for his studies.

The WoRe needs to check you your fulfillment to the criteria with written confirmation. So please bring all the needed document to the Sprechstunde.
